What is Shorts-ai?

Shorts-ai is a personal automation tool that creates short, educational "On This Day" history videos (YouTube Shorts) and publishes them to its developer's own YouTube channel, WhatHappenTodayNow.

It is a single-owner tool. It is not offered to the public, has no sign-up, and does not collect or store information about any viewer or other user.

How it works

1. TopicAn event for today's date is picked from Wikipedia's "On This Day".
2. ScriptAn AI model writes a short, factual narration.
3. VideoImages, a synthetic voice, subtitles and the calendar mascot "Cal" are rendered into a vertical video.
4. UploadThe finished video is uploaded to the owner's channel through the YouTube Data API.

Use of YouTube API Services

The app uses the YouTube Data API videos.insert method with the scope youtube.upload, only to upload videos to the developer's own channel. It does not read, download, analyze or store any YouTube data belonging to other people.

All videos are disclosed as containing AI-generated (synthetic) content, as YouTube requires. Use of YouTube is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
