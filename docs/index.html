Shorts-ai

Shorts-ai is a personal automation tool that creates short educational videos about "what happened today in history" and uploads them to its owner's own YouTube channel (WhatHappenTodayNow).

The app uses the YouTube Data API only to upload videos to the channel of its developer. It does not access, collect or store data from any other user.
