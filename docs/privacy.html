Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Who uses this app. Shorts-ai is a personal tool used only by its developer to publish videos to the developer's own YouTube channel. It is not offered to the public.

Data we access. The app requests the YouTube upload permission (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload) for the developer's own Google account, solely to upload videos that the app generates. It does not read, collect, or store personal data of any other person.

Data storage. An OAuth refresh token for the developer's account is stored as an encrypted secret in the developer's private automation settings. It is never shared, sold, or disclosed to third parties.

YouTube. The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access. Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
